SeconTrade · Agentic AI

An AI trading agent for your Robinhood account

SeconTrade researches the market, watches your positions around the clock, and brings you clear, reasoned trade ideas — but you hold the trigger on every single order.

Always-on research

Scans prices, news, and your watchlist continuously, so opportunities and risks don't slip past after hours.

Trades you approve, every time

SeconTrade only ever proposes. Nothing is bought or sold until you review the reasoning and tap approve.

Guardrails you set

Define position limits, no-trade lists, and risk budgets up front — the agent stays inside the lines you draw.

Plain-English rationale

Every idea comes with the "why" — the signals behind it and the risk — so you decide with full context.
